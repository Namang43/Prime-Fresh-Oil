### Who we are

Prime Fresh Oil Mills is a vegetable oil manufacturer. We press and process oil seeds and supply what comes out of the mill in three forms: finished oil, raw seeds and de-oiled cake. Our customers are businesses, so we work in bulk quantities and speak directly with the people who buy.

### What we make and supply

- **Oils:** yellow mustard oil, our hero product, and sesame oil.
- **Seeds:** yellow and black mustard seeds, groundnut seeds, sesame seeds and black sesame seeds.
- **De-oiled cakes:** yellow mustard cake and black mustard cake, the solids left after pressing.

### Our bestsellers

Yellow mustard oil is our best-selling oil. Groundnut seeds, sesame seeds and sesame oil are also among the products buyers ask for most. See them on the [Top sellers](#top) page.

### Who we work with

- **Edible oil brands** that need a dependable bulk source for their packed oil range.
- **FMCG companies** that want oils, seeds and cakes from one manufacturer.
- **Private-label businesses** that want their own brand on the product.

### How we work with you

- You tell us the product, quantity, unit, packing and delivery address.

## Quick answers

What is the minimum order quantity?

It depends on the product and packing. Share your quantity in the quote form and the team will confirm.

Can I get a sample first?

Yes. Request one in the quote form or on WhatsApp. Sample terms are confirmed by the team.

Do you do private label?

Private-label enquiries are welcome. Mention your brand and packing needs in the notes.

Why does single-oil supply matter for mustard oil?

Since 8 June 2021 FSSAI prohibits mustard oil in multi-sourced blended oils, so buyers should expect unblended supply with proper documents.

Which cakes do you offer?

De-oiled cakes from yellow and black mustard. Ask the team about grades and typical uses.

Which units can I order in?

Kilograms, tonnes, litres or 15 L tins. You can set a unit for each product in your quote list.
